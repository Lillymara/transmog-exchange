List your own items

The transmog_inventory Lich script reads your locker and puts your transmog items here. Prices and notes are optional.

  1. Get the script. Download transmog_inventory.lic and save it in your Lich scripts folder.
  2. Scan your items. Stand at your open locker with a free hand and type ;transmog_inventory. Do this on each character you want to include.
  3. Pick the name buyers will see. ;transmog_inventory setname YourName discord:your.handle (the Discord part is optional, but it lets people contact you).
  4. Publish. ;transmog_inventory upload. Run it again whenever your items change.

Set a price with ;transmog_inventory price <id> 25k (or free), add a note with ;transmog_inventory note <id> <text>, and take your listing down with ;transmog_inventory clearweb confirm. ;transmog_inventory help lists everything.
